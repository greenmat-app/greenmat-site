Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026 · Effective date: November 12, 2026

Overview

GreenMat is an iPhone app for golf swing analysis. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, where it is stored, and your rights.

By using GreenMat, you agree to the practices described in this policy. You may revoke your consent at any time from the in-app Settings.

Who we are

Developer: Greenmat LLC, Irvine, California, United States

Contact: contact@greenmat.app

What we collect

CategoryExamplesPurpose
Swing videosShort video clips recorded by your rear camera during a swing sessionCore app functionality and product improvement
Body movement dataMovement and position data extracted on-device during recordingSwing analysis and comparison

What we do NOT collect

  • Name, email, phone number, or contact information
  • GPS location
  • Microphone audio
  • Photos from your camera roll
  • Health or fitness data
  • Browsing history or activity in other apps
  • Any cross-app tracking

How we use your data

  1. Providing the app's core functionality: analyzing your swings and displaying results
  2. Improving the app: identifying issues and measuring performance
  3. Training machine learning models: using anonymized data to improve future versions

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, or any unrelated purpose. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on servers operated by Supabase in the United States. By using GreenMat, you consent to this transfer.

Who has access

  • You, through the GreenMat app
  • The developer, for product improvement, via a secured dashboard
  • Supabase Inc., as our data processor (their privacy policy)

We do not share your data with any other party.

Data retention

  • Active data is retained for the study duration plus 12 months
  • You may request deletion at any time by contacting contact@greenmat.app
  • We will delete your data within 30 days of your request

Your rights

  • Access: request a copy of your data
  • Delete: request deletion via email or in-app Settings
  • Withdraw consent: revoke at any time from Settings
  • Object: email us to exclude your data from specific uses

If you are in the EU, you have additional rights under GDPR. The legal basis for processing is your explicit consent (Article 6(1)(a)).

Children

GreenMat is not directed at children under 13.

Security

All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with encryption at rest. Access controls prevent users from seeing other users' data.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated in-app.
