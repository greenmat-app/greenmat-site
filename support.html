GreenMat for iPhone

Contact us

Email contact@greenmat.app. A person reads every message.

If something went wrong, it helps to include:

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • What you were doing and what you expected to happen
  • The date and rough time of the session

Camera setup

  • Use a tripod. Place the phone at hand height, about 10 feet behind your hands.
  • Point it down the target line, so the line through your hands runs toward the target.
  • Rough placement is fine.

Your data

To request a copy of your data or to have it deleted, email contact@greenmat.app with "GreenMat Privacy" in the subject line. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
